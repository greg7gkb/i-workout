Goblet Squat

Hold one dumbbell or kettlebell upright against your chest, like a goblet. Squat down between your knees, stand up. The weight up front stops you tipping forward.

2 × 1020–25 lb
lb used

Bulgarian Split Squat

Back foot up on a bench or chair, front foot a stride ahead. Lower straight down, not forward. Fingertips on a wall for balance. Bodyweight only, for months.

2 × 6each leg · no load
notes

Romanian Deadlift

Dumbbells in hand, soft knees. Push your hips back toward the wall behind you, flat back, weights sliding down your thighs. Stand by squeezing your glutes. Hinge, not squat.

2 × 8light
lb used

Dumbbell Row

One hand and one knee on a bench, other arm hanging with the dumbbell. Pull your elbow up toward your hip, like starting a lawnmower. For the hunched-over-the-bars posture.

2 × 10each side
lb used
5

Dead Bug

On your back, arms up, knees bent at 90 degrees. Slowly extend the opposite arm and leg toward the floor. Lower back stays pressed flat the whole time.

2 × 8each side
